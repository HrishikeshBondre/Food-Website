FRUITS...

Fruits are an essential part of a healthy diet, providing a wide range of nutrients, including vitamins, minerals, fiber, and antioxidants. Here's some general nutrition information about common fruits:

  1. Apples:
    • Nutrients: Rich in vitamin C and dietary fiber. Also contain small amounts of other vitamins and minerals.
    • Calories: Approximately 95 calories per medium-sized apple.

  2. Bananas:
    • Nutrients: High in potassium, vitamin B6, vitamin C, and dietary fiber.
    • Calories: About 105 calories per medium-sized banana.

  3. Oranges:
    • Nutrients: Excellent source of vitamin C, potassium, and fiber.
    • Calories: Approximately 62 calories per medium-sized orange.

  4. Strawberries:
    • Nutrients: High in vitamin C, manganese, folate, and antioxidants.
    • Calories: Around 50 calories per cup of sliced strawberries.

  5. Blueberries:
    • Nutrients: Rich in antioxidants (particularly anthocyanins), vitamin C, vitamin K, and fiber.
    • Calories: Roughly 80 calories per cup of fresh blueberries.

  6. Grapes:
    • Nutrients: Contains vitamin C, vitamin K, potassium, and various antioxidants like resveratrol.
    • Calories: About 62 calories per cup of grapes.

  7. Watermelon:
    • Nutrients: High water content, providing hydration. Also a good source of vitamin C, vitamin A, and lycopene.
    • Calories: Approximately 46 calories per cup of diced watermelon.

  8. Pineapple:
    • Nutrients: Rich in vitamin C, manganese, and bromelain (an enzyme with anti-inflammatory properties).
    • Calories: Around 82 calories per cup of diced pineapple.

  9. Mango:
    • Nutrients: High in vitamin C, vitamin A, vitamin E, and fiber.
    • Calories: Approximately 99 calories per cup of sliced mango.

  10. Avocado (technically a fruit):
    • Nutrients: High in healthy fats (monounsaturated), fiber, potassium, vitamin K, vitamin E, and folate.
    • Calories: About 234 calories per cup of sliced avocado.
