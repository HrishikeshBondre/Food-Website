MILK...

Milk is a highly nutritious beverage that provides a range of essential nutrients. Here's a general overview of the nutrition information for one cup (approximately 244 grams) of whole cow's milk:

  • Calories: 149
  • Protein: 7.7 grams
  • Fat: 7.9 grams
  • Saturated Fat: 4.6 grams
  • Monounsaturated Fat: 2.1 grams
  • Polyunsaturated Fat: 0.3 grams
  • Carbohydrates: 11.7 grams
  • Sugars: 11.7 grams (naturally occurring lactose)
  • Calcium: 276 milligrams (28% of the recommended daily intake)
  • Vitamin D: 2.9 micrograms (15% of the recommended daily intake)
  • Vitamin B12: 1.1 micrograms (46% of the recommended daily intake)
  • Riboflavin (Vitamin B2): 0.3 milligrams (22% of the recommended daily intake)
  • Phosphorus: 205 milligrams (16% of the recommended daily intake)
  • Potassium: 322 milligrams (9% of the recommended daily intake)

These values are approximate and may vary slightly depending on factors such as the brand of milk and any fortification it may have undergone. Additionally, milk is a good source of other essential nutrients such as vitamin A, vitamin B6, magnesium, and zinc. It's worth noting that the nutritional composition may vary for different types of milk, such as skim milk (which has lower fat content), 2% milk, or plant-based alternatives like almond milk, soy milk, or oat milk.
