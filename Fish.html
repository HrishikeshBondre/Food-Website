Fishes...

TFish is a highly nutritious food packed with essential nutrients. Here's a general overview of the nutrition information for a 3-ounce (85-gram) serving of cooked fish, particularly focusing on commonly consumed varieties such as salmon, tuna, and cod:

  1. Salmon (wild-caught, cooked):
    • Calories: 155
    • Protein: 22 grams
    • Fat: 7 grams
    • Saturated Fat: 1.3 grams
    • Monounsaturated Fat: 2.7 grams
    • Omega-3 Fatty Acids: 1.8 grams
    • Omega-6 Fatty Acids: 0.5 grams
    • Vitamin D: 570 IU (142% of the recommended daily intake)
    • Vitamin B12: 2.8 micrograms (117% of the recommended daily intake)
    • Selenium: 31 micrograms (56% of the recommended daily intake)

  2. Tuna (light, canned in water, drained):
    • Calories: 99
    • Protein: 22 grams
    • Fat: 0.9 grams
    • Saturated Fat: 0.2 grams
    • Monounsaturated Fat: 0.2 grams
    • Polyunsaturated Fat: 0.3 grams
    • Omega-3 Fatty Acids: 0.2 grams
    • Omega-6 Fatty Acids: 0.1 grams
    • Vitamin D: 17 IU (3% of the recommended daily intake)
    • Vitamin B12: 1.7 micrograms (71% of the recommended daily intake)
    • Niacin (Vitamin B3): 12.3 milligrams (77% of the recommended daily intake)

  3. Cod (cooked):
    • Calories: 89
    • Protein: 20 grams
    • Fat: 0.7 grams
    • Saturated Fat: 0.1 grams
    • Monounsaturated Fat: 0.1 grams
    • Polyunsaturated Fat: 0.2 grams
    • Omega-3 Fatty Acids: 0.2 grams
    • Omega-6 Fatty Acids: 0.1 grams
    • Vitamin D: 4 IU (1% of the recommended daily intake)
    • Vitamin B12: 1.1 micrograms (45% of the recommended daily intake)
    • Selenium: 31 micrograms (56% of the recommended daily intake)

Fish is also a good source of other nutrients such as iodine, phosphorus, potassium, and various B vitamins. Additionally, fatty fish like salmon are particularly rich in omega-3 fatty acids, which are beneficial for heart health and brain function. It's important to note that nutritional values may vary slightly depending on factors such as cooking method and specific species of fish.
