Egg...

Eggs are highly nutritious and provide a range of essential nutrients. Here's the nutrition information for a single large egg (approximately 50 grams), including both the yolk and the egg white:

  • Calories: 71
  • Protein: 6.3 grams
  • Fat: 4.8 grams
  • Saturated Fat: 1.6 grams
  • Monounsaturated Fat: 2.0 grams
  • Polyunsaturated Fat: 0.7 grams
  • Cholesterol: 186 milligrams (62% of the recommended daily intake)
  • Carbohydrates: 0.4 grams
  • Sugars: 0.2 grams
  • Vitamin A: 75 micrograms (8% of the recommended daily intake)
  • Vitamin D: 1.1 micrograms (6% of the recommended daily intake)
  • Vitamin E: 0.5 milligrams (3% of the recommended daily intake)
  • Vitamin B2 (Riboflavin): 0.2 milligrams (15% of the recommended daily intake)
  • Vitamin B12: 0.6 micrograms (25% of the recommended daily intake)
  • Folate: 24 micrograms (6% of the recommended daily intake)
  • Calcium: 28 milligrams (2% of the recommended daily intake)
  • Iron: 0.9 milligrams (5% of the recommended daily intake)
  • Phosphorus: 86 milligrams (7% of the recommended daily intake)
  • Potassium: 69 milligrams (1% of the recommended daily intake)
  • Selenium: 15.4 micrograms (28% of the recommended daily intake)

Eggs are also rich in other essential nutrients such as choline, biotin, and various antioxidants. It's important to note that the nutrient content may vary slightly depending on factors such as the size of the egg and the diet of the hen. Additionally, most of the vitamins and minerals are found in the yolk, while the egg white is primarily composed of protein.
